What I did.

Integrated.

Applied derivative-free optimization and CMA-ES ideas to lower-limb exoskeleton control work using NVIDIA Jetson hardware.

Programmed.

Built.

Designed a 13s1p 48 V, 433 Wh battery pack using Molicel P45B cells, integrated a JKBMS, and packaged the system in a custom printed enclosure for a 1000 W hub-motor bicycle.
